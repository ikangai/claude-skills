# Changelog

All notable changes to the omakase skill are documented here.

## 2026-09-06 (v2)

### Added

- **The principle, stated.** SKILL.md now opens with the one-paragraph Omakase
  principle: agree on the outcome, the constraints, and what counts as evidence
  of success; the agent chooses the implementation and makes its consequential
  choices visible; taste is settled with concrete alternatives. The eight steps
  are framed as chosen practices, not derivations.
- **Success and evidence lines.** The menu now opens with what success means
  and how it will be checked, and the closing omakase card repeats both with
  what the check actually showed.
- **Principle boundaries** in `references/principles.md`: method-is-the-product
  domains (regulated, safety-critical, audited) are out of scope, and a menu
  makes assumptions contestable, not safe.

### Changed

- **Questions are sorted by kind, not counted.** Step 1 replaces "exactly one
  question" with "ask about the outcome, never about the method."
- **Review is not evidence.** Step 6 runs the evidence check first and treats
  the independent reader as an extra layer.
- **Step 7 is bounded.** Physics-chasing applies only when the user named a
  number; the backlog ends at that number, and going past it is a tasting to
  offer. The physical floor is computed to tell whether the number is
  reachable. The physics section of `principles.md` now says the same.
- **Step 8 offers a repo and releases instead of creating them unasked.**
- Dropped the undefined `[stated]` tag from step 2.

## 2026-09-06 (v1)
